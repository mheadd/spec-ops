What Is the SpecOps Community?

SpecOps is a community of practice for people working on legacy system modernization in government. We share techniques, tools, and hard-won lessons about using AI and specification-driven approaches to preserve institutional knowledge while building modern systems.

Whether you're staring down a COBOL mainframe, wrestling with undocumented business rules, or trying to modernize a critical system without breaking it — you're in the right place.

👥 Who It's For

  • Government technologists modernizing legacy systems
  • Domain experts who understand the policy and business rules embedded in legacy code
  • Policy people responsible for the rules these systems implement
  • Civic technologists building tools and approaches for public-sector modernization

🎁 What Members Get

  • Slack workspace — Connect with peers, ask questions, and share what you're learning
  • GitHub organization — Access shared tools, templates, and reference implementations
  • Free book — Founding members receive a complimentary copy of the SpecOps book

💬 What We Talk About

  • Strategies for understanding and documenting legacy systems
  • Using AI as a force multiplier for modernization
  • Writing specifications that domain experts can verify
  • Sharing instruction sets and patterns across agencies
